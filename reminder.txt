Recommendation Page

. Splits into two layers that deliberately do different jobs:
    Layer 1 - a weighted risk score (0–100) 
            - answers "how worried should we be about this SKU stocking out?" 
            - It's a continuous number, good for ranking.
    Layer 2 — gating rules answer "why can't procurement proceed, and what's the actual next action?" 
            - These override the score, 
            - because an expired registration isn't "high risk" — it's a categorically different situation where placing a PO would be the wrong move entirely.

Layer 1: The factor registry and risk score
Layer 2: The gating ladder
